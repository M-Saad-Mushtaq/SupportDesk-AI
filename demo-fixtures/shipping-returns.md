# Fictional Demo Store Policies

This fixture is fictional portfolio demonstration data, not a real store policy.
It is not part of the active Knowledge Base until the demo owner imports it.

## Returns
Unused items may be returned within 30 days with proof of purchase. A support
specialist reviews damaged or faulty items and any replacement or refund request.
No refund or replacement is performed by this demo.

## Shipping
Standard shipping in this fictional store takes five business days after dispatch.
Weather can delay delivery. For a time-sensitive issue, request human support.
Stored demo order tracking is sample information, not a live carrier update.
